================================================================================
                                   ABSTRACT
================================================================================
Industrial manufacturing and assembly environments rely heavily on millions of
mechanical fasteners such as nuts, bolts, screws, and washers. In traditional
workshops and assembly floors, fastener sorting, segregation, and quality 
inspection are performed manually—a labor-intensive, error-prone, and slow 
process that significantly diminishes productivity and introduces human error into 
quality control pipelines.

This project proposes an intelligent, real-time "AI-Powered Automated Fastener 
Inspection and Sorting System" that combines high-speed Computer Vision (OpenCV) 
with Multimodal Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini Vision API) and an 
industrial-grade desktop operator dashboard (PySide6 / Qt). The system acquires live 
video feeds of mechanical fasteners, isolates regions of interest (ROI), extracts 
geometric and spatial features (such as aspect ratio, circularity, solidity, convexity, 
internal hole detection, and tip profiles), and accurately classifies components into 
discrete categories (Hex Nuts, Hex Bolts, Wood/Machine Screws, Flat Washers, and 
Foreign Objects) with over 96% confidence.

The dual-engine architecture guarantees high availability: it delivers zero-latency 
offline classification via local geometric computer vision algorithms while 
seamlessly offering cloud multimodal AI inference for advanced engineering 
diagnostics. Real-time batch counters, visual bounding-box overlays, and automated 
CSV audit logs provide end-to-end traceability for modern Industry 4.0 applications.


================================================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
Fasteners are fundamental mechanical components used across automotive, aerospace, 
civil, electronics, and heavy machinery industries. A typical production facility 
handles hundreds of distinct fastener variations daily, differing in thread pitch, 
diameter, length, head configuration, and driving recess.

Manual segregation of mixed fasteners is monotonous, fatigue-inducing, and 
inefficient. Human operators often misidentify fasteners of similar diameters or 
misplace damaged components onto high-speed assembly lines, resulting in structural 
defects, machine downtime, and costly product recalls.

Recent advancements in embedded vision systems and computer vision algorithms have 
made automated visual inspection commercially viable. By deploying camera sensors 
coupled with algorithmic image processing, an automated sorting system can 
inspect fasteners at high throughput without physical contact. 

This project demonstrates a comprehensive, robust inspection solution engineered 
for high accuracy, visual auditability, offline resilience, and expandable 
pneumatic/mechanical sorting actuator integration.

2.1 PROBLEM STATEMENT:
----------------------
"To design and develop an automated, vision-based artificial intelligence system 
capable of real-time detection, feature extraction, accurate classification, and 
audit tracking of mixed industrial mechanical fasteners (Hex Nuts, Hex Bolts, 
Screws, and Washers) using computer vision and multimodal AI models, eliminating 
manual sorting errors and establishing automated quality logging."


2.2 OBJECTIVES:
---------------
1. Real-Time Vision Pipeline:
   Develop a non-blocking 30+ FPS video streaming interface with automated frame 
   capture and region-of-interest (ROI) contour segmentation.

2. Dual-Engine Classification Architecture:
   - Primary: Offline Computer Vision Engine utilizing geometric heuristics 
     (aspect ratio, contour area, solidity, circularity, inner-hole topology, 
     and tip sharpness) for instant zero-latency classification without internet.
   - Secondary: Cloud Multimodal AI (Google Gemini Vision API) for zero-shot 
     deep contextual analysis and automated engineering defect explanations.

3. Graphical Operator Interface (HMI):
   Build an industrial-grade Desktop GUI (PySide6/Qt) providing real-time video 
   rendering, annotated bounding boxes, category counters, and system health status.

4. Audit Logging & Export:
   Maintain real-time timestamped inspection logs with automatic batch counting 
   and CSV export capability for industrial compliance and quality assurance.

5. Actuator Compatibility (Future Extension):
   Provide modular software interfaces for microcontrollers (Arduino / ESP32) 
   to trigger servo/pneumatic diverter gates for automated physical sorting bins.

3.2 BRIEF EXPLANATION OF WORKING:
---------------------------------
1. Image Acquisition:
   A high-resolution optical camera captures frames of fasteners positioned on a 
   conveyor or static inspection pad. The `camera.py` module manages acquisition 
   in a dedicated background `QThread` to prevent GUI stutters.

2. Preprocessing & Segmentation:
   The captured frame is converted to grayscale, filtered via Gaussian blur, and 
   segmented using Otsu's adaptive thresholding. Morphological operations remove 
   spurious noise and isolate individual fastener contours.

3. Feature Extraction:
   The system extracts key geometric descriptors:
   - Inner Hole Topology: Detects presence of internal hollow rings (Nuts & Washers).
   - Circularity & Compactness: Differentiates faceted Hex Nuts from smooth Washers.
   - Aspect Ratio: Measures elongation (Bolts & Screws vs Nuts & Washers).
   - Tip Profile: Evaluates diameter ratios at the ends to distinguish flat, blunt 
     Bolt ends from tapered, pointed Screw tips.

4. Intelligent Classification:
   - In Local Mode, mathematical decision-tree rules classify the fastener in <5ms.
   - In Cloud AI Mode, the cropped ROI image is encoded in Base64/JPEG and submitted 
     to the Gemini multimodal API with an enforced JSON schema response.

5. Visual Display, Counting & Auditing:
   The UI renders the live feed with color-coded bounding boxes and confidence 
   badges, increments the corresponding category batch counter, and appends the 
   record into an exportable CSV history table.


3.3 APPLICATIONS OF PROPOSED SYSTEM:
------------------------------------
1. Automotive Assembly Lines:
   Verifying correct fastener specifications before automated robotic tightening.

2. Hardware & Fastener Manufacturing Units:
   Post-machining automated sorting, counting, and packaging into retail boxes.

3. Aviation & Aerospace Maintenance (MRO):
   Sorting mixed bins of high-precision aviation fasteners during teardown.

4. Recycling & Scrap Metal Recovery:
   Segregating high-grade fasteners from scrap metal bins automatically.

5. Warehouses & Inventory Management:
   Rapid stock auditing and automated count reconciliation.

   4. Computer Vision Library: OpenCV (opencv-python 4.10+ / 5.0+).
   5. Numerical Processing: NumPy (numpy 1.26+).
   6. Image Utility: Pillow (PIL 10.0+).
   7. Cloud AI SDK: google-generativeai / google-genai.
   8. Environment Config: python-dotenv.
